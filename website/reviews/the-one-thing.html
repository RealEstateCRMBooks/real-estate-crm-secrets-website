The ONE Thing: The Surprisingly Simple Truth Behind Extraordinary Results

By Gary Keller, Jay Papasan

This is an extraordinary book for agents looking to grow in the real estate business. Through simple explanations, the authors lay out common misconceptions about working in real estate as well as the realities of being an agent. The book breaks down fallacies, such as the idea that discipline, willpower, and multitasking are all you need to be successful, and then shows how strong habits, productivity, and prioritization are the real tools for generating more sales.

A lot of real estate books point out tips for connecting with clients or persuading them to make a purchase, but this book is more focused on prioritization and developing the work ethic needed to get results. I found Part 3 of the book to be particularly insightful with its focus on realistic changes you can make to become a better agent. You can be highly motivated and disciplined but still fail to achieve your goals if your productivity is out of alignment. The authors’ ONE Thing philosophy helps agents focus on what truly needs to get done without becoming overwhelmed by everything else competing for their attention.

If you’ve read Real Estate CRM Secrets, you’ll recognize this same discipline in its Morning Map, Evening Wrap secret — checking your calendar and tasks list each morning and evening to stay accountable for your obligations, while a well-kept contacts database helps you maintain accurate records and reminders of important details. The book’s other habits, like relying on pre-made mailings and customizable flyers, make communication faster, allowing you to focus on other responsibilities. If you’re an agent looking to sharpen your focus, increase sales, and grow your business, The ONE Thing can help you get there.
